Forkleaf Support

Forkleaf helps you find vegan choices on restaurant menus around Needham, Massachusetts. Coverage and recipes change, so always confirm ingredients and shared equipment with staff.

Contact

Email keith@arcemdigitalsolutions.com with the restaurant, the dish, your app version and what happened. Please don't include passwords, financial details or sensitive personal information.

Common questions

A dish is labeled wrong. Open the restaurant and choose “This is wrong.” Corrections currently stay on your iPhone, so email us too if you'd like it fixed for everyone.

Does it work offline? Saved menus work offline. Connect to the internet for live discovery and restaurant pages.

Location isn't available. Choose “Use Needham, MA.”

A craving shows no results. Clear the craving, or widen the radius to five miles.

Menus won't open. Close and reopen Forkleaf.

Forkleaf labels come from menu text and curated notes. It does not guarantee that a meal is vegan or allergen-free. Tell restaurant staff about allergies before ordering.
