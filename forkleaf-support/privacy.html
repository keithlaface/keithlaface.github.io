Forkleaf Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026. Forkleaf is made by Arcem Digital Solutions (Arcem LLC).

Forkleaf helps you find vegan dishes at nearby restaurants. It has no advertising, no analytics SDK, no tracking and no account requirement.

What stays on your iPhone

Profiles, diet preferences, allergy reminders, favorites and corrections are stored locally. Rules classify menu text on your device. When available, Apple's on-device model may add caution to an uncertain dish; it cannot upgrade a dish to vegan. Forkleaf does not upload these preferences and corrections. Local data may be included in backups controlled by your device settings.

Location and network requests

Location is optional and requested only while you use the app. It supports nearby searches, map centering and distance calculations. Without permission, you can explore the included Needham collection.

Location is not used only on your device. Live restaurant searches send a location-centered search region to Apple Maps. OpenStreetMap's public Overpass service receives the center of a coarse search tile about two kilometres across. Map rendering also uses Apple services. Forkleaf has no publisher-operated location server and does not upload your location history to us. Service providers receive normal network information, including an IP address, and their own policies govern their processing and retention.

Forkleaf requests menu pages from restaurant websites and permitted menu hosting sites. Those hosts receive ordinary web request information. Links and directions open the relevant external service. Forkleaf does not attach profile names, allergies, favorites, corrections or advertising identifiers to these requests.

Storage and control

Menus and nearby search responses are cached on your device. Menu caches are reused for up to 30 days and nearby search caches for up to one day. These are freshness windows, not promises that files are erased at expiry. Local files remain until replaced, removed by iOS or the app is deleted. Delete a profile in the app, or delete Forkleaf to remove its local app data; device backups are managed separately. You can revoke location permission in iOS Settings.
